# Glossary

_> If you have [specialized vocabulary definitions](https://apistyleguide.cisco.com/#apix-documentation/learn-more-section) that are crucial to understanding your API design, include them in this section for reference. Below are some examples from SD-WAN that may be used for inspiration and formatting purposes:_

* Domain ID
A domain is a logical grouping of vEdge routers and vSmart controllers that demarcates the span of control for the vSmart controllers. Each domain is identified by a unique integer, called the domain ID. Currently, you can configure only one domain in a Cisco SD-WAN overlay network.

* OMP Routes
On vSmart controllers and vEdge routers, OMP advertises to its peers the routes and services that it has learned from its local site, along with their corresponding transport location mappings, which are called TLOCs. These routes are called OMP routes, to distinguish them from standard IP routes. It is through OMP routes that the vSmart controllers learn the network topology and the available services.
